= Crispy folded pastry stuffed with egg, minced meat and leek.
- 250 g flour
- 150 ml water
- 200 g beef mince
- 1 leek, finely chopped
- 3 eggs
- 1 tsp baharat
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
* Knead the flour and water into a smooth dough and rest for 15 minutes.
* Brown the mince with the leek and baharat for 7 minutes. Cool, then mix with the beaten eggs.
* Stretch the dough very thinly, add the filling and fold into a square parcel.
* Fry in the oil for 3 minutes per side until crisp. Cut into squares.

@ Balaleet with Saffron Eggs | Kuwaiti | breakfast | 3 | 5 | 15 | noodles | vegetarian
= Sweet saffron vermicelli topped with a thin savoury omelette.
- 200 g vermicelli
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1 pinch saffron
- ½ tsp ground cardamom
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 eggs
* Boil the vermicelli for 3 minutes and drain.
* Melt the butter, add the vermicelli, sugar, saffron and cardamom and stir for 4 minutes.
* Whisk the eggs and cook as a thin omelette.
* Pile the vermicelli on plates and top with strips of omelette.
